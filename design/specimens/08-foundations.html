型 Kata — Kimono foundations

The catalog phase produced eleven metaphors. A system cannot carry eleven. This is the cut: three materials, four depths, three interaction primitives, and fixed scales. Anything not on this page does not exist.

墨紙木Three materials

Every surface in Kimono is paper, every mark is ink, every physical object is wood. Signal colours are not materials — they are applied to them. If a colour is not on this row it is not in the system.

Accent is never decoration. It marks a thing that wants you, or an action you can take. Moss means running and nothing else. Nothing is ever coloured to be pretty.

深Four depths, expressed as edges

Kimono has no blurred shadows and no elevation ramp. Depth is edge weight: hairline, rule, frame. A tray may carry one hard offset shadow, never a blur. There are exactly four depths and no fifth.

三Three interaction primitives

Every interactive thing in Kimono is one of these three. If a need does not fit, it is text and a seal — not a new object.

障子Door

Goes somewhere. Only ever navigation. Lives in chrome, never in content.

継手Joint

Turns something on or off. The only switch. Apart is off, seated is on.

判Seal

Commits an action, or states a fact. Pressed it stamps; static it labels a state.

客Opening an app

Kimono hosts apps it did not write. It cannot restyle Outline, and should not try. So the system's job at the moment you open one is to own the frame and the crossing, then get out of the way. The rail is the only thing Kimono draws: the door back, the app's bloom, its name, its reach, its address. Everything under the rule belongs to the guest.

Outline Public outline.house.net

The guest application renders here, in its own design language.
Kimono contributes nothing below the rule — no fonts, no colours, no chrome.

The rail is 52px, one rule thick, and never scrolls away. It is the only place a door may appear inside an app.
