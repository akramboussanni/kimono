App cards — sheet 05

Three ways an app can present itself, each shown twice: as the household sees it, and as you see it in admin where private and disabled have to be unmissable. Every concept encodes state in form — position, light, angle — not only colour. Each app carries its own sprig as a crest, and alerts stamp the top-right corner.

暖簾Noren — the shop curtain

A noren hangs out when the shop is open and comes in when it closes — the most literal status object in the vocabulary. Panels parted means you can walk in from the street; closed means the house only; no curtain at all means nothing is running. The app's sprig is its crest, printed on the cloth the way a shop's 紋 always is.

提灯Chōchin — the paper lantern

Lit or unlit, readable across a room and across a grid. An unlit lantern also slumps, so the state survives greyscale. Lanterns carry the crest too, so the sprig sits on the paper.

木札Kifuda — the wooden tag

The key-board tag at an inn: face-out when the room is in use, knocked askew when it is not. Densest of the three, so it is the only one that would survive a forty-app admin list.
