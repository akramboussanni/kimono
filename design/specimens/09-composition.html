型 said depth is edge weight. It did not say when to use which, so everything got the heavy edge and nothing was ranked. This is the missing law, and the answer to why the current admin panel feels arbitrary.

何故Why edges at all

Honestly: because you asked for thick borders, and it worked. The justification came after, but it holds — paper stacks, it does not glow. A drop shadow claims a surface is floating above another in space, which is a screen fiction; a visible edge claims one sheet is laid on another, which is what actually happens on a desk. Once that is the model, blur has no meaning and edge weight carries everything.

The mistake was applying one weight to everything. A 3px frame around a passive region and a 2px border on a button say the same thing at the same volume, so the eye cannot tell what it may touch.

三重The law: weight is touchability

Three weights, and the rule that assigns them is not decorative — it is can I touch this.

3px frame — inkThe outer boundary of a workspace. One per screen region. Never nested inside another frame.
2px rule — inkAnything you can touch: controls, switches, seals. And the seams between compartments of a tray.
1px hairline — hairPassive division inside a compartment. Separates, never encloses.

Corollaries, all mechanical: edges are shared, never doubled — adjacent compartments meet on one seam. A hairline never touches a frame; if they would meet, drop the hairline. Frames never nest.
