Button specimens — sheet 01

Kimono's own palette — sumi ink, paper, and the ema plaque's wood. Hover, press and toggle each one; several only show what they do once you use them.

  • 01Hanko

    Pressing leaves an impression that lingers.

  • 02Noren

    The curtain splits the label; you part it to pass through.

  • 03Shoji

    A crack on approach, all the way open if you stay — or click and it goes straight there.

  • Run this app

    04Tsugite — switch

    Apart is off. Click and the joint drives home and stays seated.

  • 05Cord

    Pull it down and it stays down. Position is the state.

  • 06Mizuhiki

    The bow cinches into a knot that cannot be retied.

  • 07Brush

    Loaded at the start of the stroke, running dry at the tail.

  • 08Approach

    Reads how fast you came at it. Rush it and it snaps.

  • 09Kintsugi

    The break stays visible until you mend it.

  • 10Fold

    A folded corner that opens out as you reach it.

  • 11Wear

    Keeps a record of every press it has taken.

  • 12Hold

    Petals fill it left to right while you hold. Let go early and they scatter.

  • 13Bell

    Struck, then left to settle on its own.

  • 14Ma

    Makes room for itself as you approach.

04 Tsugite — joint profiles

Same switch, three real joints. The profile is not decoration: a dovetail cannot be pulled apart in the direction it was slid together, which is a different promise from a tenon — so the joint can say how reversible the setting is.

    04aAri — dovetail

    Flares outward. Slots in, will not pull back out.

  • Run this app

    04bHozo — tenon

    The straight tab you already saw. Plainest of the three.

    04cAikaki — half-lap

    The pieces step past each other rather than interlocking.
